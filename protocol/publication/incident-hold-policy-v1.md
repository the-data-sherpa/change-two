# Incident Hold Policy 1.0.0

An Incident Hold preserves a named restricted Evidence Bundle beyond its normal deletion workflow only when a documented security, privacy, provenance, or evaluator-integrity incident requires investigation.

The hold record must identify the bundle checksum, incident, approving reviewer, start time, review date, and access list. The approving reviewer must not be the person who requests the hold. Review the hold at least every 14 days.

Do not use a hold for convenience, future analysis, or indefinite archiving. Close it when the named investigation no longer needs the raw bundle. Record closure and apply Retention Policy 1.0.0 immediately.
